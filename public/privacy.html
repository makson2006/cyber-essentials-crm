  • Airtable: holds our enquiry list and customer records.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on a UK adequacy arrangement or the ICO-approved safeguards (the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum). We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a customer, we keep records as long as the law requires for accounting purposes. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days. If you ask us to stop marketing, we keep your name and contact details on a suppression list so we do not contact you again.
